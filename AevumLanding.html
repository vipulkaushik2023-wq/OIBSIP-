Who We Are

The Aevum Story

Rooted in the pursuit of lasting quality and understated luxury.

Crafted for those who value the enduring over the fleeting.

Aevum is a menswear brand dedicated to refined essentials — crisp shirts, impeccably tailored outerwear, and thoughtfully designed trousers that form the foundation of a sophisticated wardrobe.

Every piece is designed with attention to detail, premium materials, and a quiet confidence that speaks for itself. From the boardroom to the weekend, Aevum pieces are made to last and to elevate.

Founder

Vipul Kaushik

I’m studying BSc Information Technology at Baba Saheb Bhimrao University, Lucknow. Aevum is my own brand — born from a passion for timeless style and a desire to create clothing that feels both modern and enduring.

Through this platform I aim to share the Aevum vision and connect with people who appreciate quality menswear.
